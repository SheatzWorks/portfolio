## 2. ENGAGEMENT STRUCTURE

### 2.1 Statements of Work
All Services are performed under individual **Statements of Work (SoW)** referencing this Agreement. Each SoW specifies:
- Scope and deliverables
- Timeline and milestones

In case of conflict: (1) SoW, (2) This Agreement, (3) Any amendment.

Studio is an independent contractor. No employment, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship is created. Studio controls the means and methods of performing Services.

**This licence does NOT extend to:**

- Future versions/improvements of Studio IP not delivered under this Agreement

### 7.1 Term

Either party may terminate with **30 days' written notice**. SoWs in progress complete per their terms unless mutually agreed.

### 7.3 Termination for Cause

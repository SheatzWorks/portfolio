## 2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

### 2.1 Definition
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by a Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), whether oral, written, electronic, or visual, that is:
- Marked "Confidential," "Proprietary," or similar; OR
- Reasonably understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure.

### 2.2 Includes (Non-Exhaustive)
- Business plans, strategies, financials, metrics, roadmaps
- Technical data: architectures, algorithms, source code, APIs, schemas, designs
- Product plans, unreleased features, prototypes
- Customer lists, pipeline, pricing, contracts
- Trade secrets, know-how, methodologies
- Personal data (as defined by applicable privacy laws)
- This Agreement's terms and existence

### 2.3 Exclusions
Information is NOT Confidential if it:
(a) Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of Receiving Party
(b) Was known to Receiving Party prior to disclosure (documented)
(c) Is independently developed by Receiving Party without use of Confidential Information
(d) Is rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligation
(e) Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order (subject to Section 4)

## 3. OBLIGATIONS

### 3.1 Standard of Care
Receiving Party shall:
- Protect Confidential Information with **at least the same degree of care** it uses for its own similar information (minimum: reasonable care)
- Restrict access to personnel with **legitimate need-to-know** bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement
- Use Confidential Information **solely for the Purpose**
- Not reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble any Confidential Information

### 3.2 Permitted Disclosures
Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to:
- Employees, contractors, advisors with need-to-know, bound by written confidentiality obligations **no less protective** than this Agreement
- Legal/financial advisors bound by professional confidentiality
- As required by law (see Section 4)

### 3.3 Prohibited Actions
Receiving Party shall NOT:
- Use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the Purpose
- Copy Confidential Information beyond what is necessary for the Purpose
- Disclose to competitors or publish
- Remove or alter proprietary notices

## 4. REQUIRED DISCLOSURE

If Receiving Party is legally compelled to disclose Confidential Information:
1. **Promptly notify** Disclosing Party (unless prohibited by law)
2. **Cooperate** with Disclosing Party's efforts to obtain protective order/narrow disclosure
3. **Disclose only** the portion legally required
4. **Request confidential treatment** by the receiving authority

### 5.3 Residuals
